A dated record of design decisions and ideas that are the project owner's **own
original contributions**. It is kept separate from public and third-party material
(pyCycle/OpenMDAO code, NASA examples, ICAO/EASA data), which is listed in
[tools.md](tools.md) and [assumptions.md](assumptions.md).

**Rule:** if an entry could be a novel, patentable idea (new blade geometry, cycle
configuration, cooling or optimisation method), **do not commit or push it to the public
repository**. Keep it private and consult a patent attorney first. See CLAUDE.md, "IP and provenance".
